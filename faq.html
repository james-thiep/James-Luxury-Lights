Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our luxury lighting products and services

Products

What materials are used in your lights?

Our lights are crafted from premium materials including crystal, brass, copper, glass, and high-quality metals. Each material is carefully selected for its durability and aesthetic appeal. We source our materials from reputable suppliers around the world to ensure the highest quality standards.

Are your lights energy efficient?

Yes, all our lights are designed with energy efficiency in mind. We use LED technology and energy-saving components to ensure minimal power consumption while maintaining optimal lighting quality. Our LED bulbs typically use 75% less energy than traditional incandescent lighting, helping you save on electricity bills while reducing your carbon footprint.

Do you offer custom lighting solutions?

Absolutely! We specialize in creating custom lighting solutions tailored to your specific needs and design preferences. Our team of designers can work with you to create unique pieces that perfectly complement your space. Contact our customer service team to discuss your custom lighting requirements.

Shipping

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we offer international shipping to most countries. Shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on the destination. Please contact us for specific shipping information to your location. For large or delicate items, we ensure special packaging to guarantee safe delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Local deliveries within Kampala typically take 2-3 business days. For other locations in Uganda, delivery times range from 3-5 business days. For international orders, delivery times range from 5-14 business days depending on the destination and customs clearance. You'll receive a tracking number once your order is shipped.

How are fragile lighting products packaged for shipping?

We take extra care when packaging our lighting products, especially delicate items like crystal chandeliers. Each product is individually wrapped in protective materials such as foam, bubble wrap, and sturdy corrugated cardboard. For especially fragile items, we use custom-designed packaging to ensure they arrive in perfect condition.

Installation

Do you provide installation services?

Yes, we offer professional installation services for all our lighting products. Our team of certified electricians ensures proper and safe installation according to local regulations. Installation services are available within Kampala and surrounding areas. For other locations, we can recommend trusted partners who can assist with installation.

Can I install the lights myself?

While some of our products come with detailed installation instructions, we recommend professional installation for optimal performance and safety, especially for complex fixtures like chandeliers and recessed lighting. Our team is available to assist with any installation queries and can provide guidance if you choose to install the products yourself.

What's included in your installation service?

Our installation service includes mounting the fixture, connecting the wiring, testing functionality, and cleaning up after installation. For complex installations, we also provide electrical load assessments to ensure your home's electrical system can support the new lighting. All installations are carried out according to national safety standards and include a post-installation inspection.

Warranty

What warranty do you offer on your products?

All our products come with a standard 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and electrical components. Premium collections come with an extended 5-year warranty. LED light fixtures have a separate warranty specifically for the LED components, typically 3-5 years depending on the product line.

How do I claim warranty?

To claim warranty, please contact our customer service team with your order number and a description of the issue. You may need to provide photos or videos of the problem. If the product is covered under warranty, we'll arrange for repair, replacement, or refund at our discretion. For products installed by our team, we offer expedited warranty service.

Returns

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for most products in their original packaging and unused condition. Custom-made and special order items cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

How do I initiate a return?

To initiate a return, please contact our customer service team within 30 days of receiving your order. You'll need to provide your order number and reason for return. Once approved, we'll send you return instructions and a return authorization number. Items must be carefully packaged in their original packaging to prevent damage during return shipping.

Still Have Questions?

Our customer service team is here to help. Please don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or concerns.
